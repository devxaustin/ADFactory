Rewrite of a text adventure I wrote for a Java class two years ago. Built with Phaser 3 and TypeScript, with placeholder art for now.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, E or Space to interact.

Working so far: all five areas, walking, transitions, inventory, dialogue. Next up: the item trades, wood chopping and the raft ending.
